Our Story

The Best Japanese
Food in West Texas

Since 2005, Yamagata Japanese Steak House has been Lubbock's hidden gem for authentic hibachi dining. Family owned and operated by Ken and his wife, we bring the art of Japanese teppanyaki to the heart of West Texas.

Our chefs don't just cook your meal — they put on a show. From sizzling hibachi stations to fresh sushi rolls, every visit is an experience worth remembering. Generous portions, warm hospitality, and flavors that keep our guests coming back.
